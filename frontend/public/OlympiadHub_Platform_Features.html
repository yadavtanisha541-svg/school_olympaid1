🌟 Executive Summary

OlympiadHub is an all-in-one digital Olympiad preparation, examination, and diagnostics ecosystem tailored for students from Class 1 to Class 12. It supports 6 core National and International Olympiads (IMO, ISO, ICSO, IEO, IGKO, ISSO) with live computer-based tests, comprehensive syllabus kits, downloadable PDF papers with master answer keys, detailed cutoff benchmarking, and automated administration tools for schools and super administrators.

1. Student Learning & Examination Portal
Interactive student dashboard designed for self-paced mastery, daily testing, and competitive rank benchmarking.
1 Subject-wise Mock Test Series Covers
Organized by Subject & Class with dedicated series covers (e.g. Class 6 - All India IMO Mock Test Series) containing multiple full-length timed tests (Mock 1, 2, 3...) matching official Olympiad standards.
2 Live Examination Portal & Countdown Timer
High-performance online exam interface featuring automatic countdown timer (MM:SS), 1..50 question palette with status colors (Answered, Skipped, Flagged, Non-visited), section switcher, and anti-cheating controls.
3 Scorecard & In-depth Solution Analysis
Instant scorecards showcasing Time Taken, Marks Obtained, and Last Year Cutoff Marks benchmarks, coupled with 2x2 filter radios (All, Correct, Wrong, Unattempted) and detailed step-by-step explanations.
4 Previous Year Papers (PYQ) & Solutions
Authentic 5 to 10-year official past papers for IMO, ISO, ICSO, IEO, IGKO, and ISSO with verified answer keys, explanation notes, and printable question papers.
5 Official Printable PDF Question Papers
One-click generation of printable/downloadable question papers and complete master answer sheets formatted for offline study and mock exam simulations at home.
6 Adaptive Test Generator Pro
Allows students to customize practice tests by selecting subject, specific syllabus chapters, difficulty level (Easy, Medium, HOTS), question count, and test duration.
7 Daily 5-Minute Free Sprint Quizzes
Bite-sized daily subject quizzes designed to build consistent revision habits with instant scoring, hints, and daily leaderboards.
8 Revision Vault (Formula Sheets & Mind Maps)
High-yield downloadable revision summaries, speed math formula sheets, science concept mind maps, and grammar rules for last-minute exam review.

3. Super Admin Management Studio
Comprehensive administrative control panel for curriculum authors, publishers, and platform administrators.
⚡ Full-Page Model Test Paper Studio
Dedicated authoring suite to create, edit, and publish papers with custom sections, question points, timing, total marks, and target grade levels.
📋 Smart Bulk Question Importer
Paste multi-question text blocks from Word/PDFs. The parser automatically structures question text, options (A, B, C, D), correct answers, marks, and explanations.
🎯 Cutoff Marks Configurator
Set national benchmark cutoff marks for any mock test paper (e.g. 42 / 60) which dynamically populates on the student's post-exam scorecard.
📦 Study Package & Bundle Builder
Create paid and premium study packs, set pricing, original strike-through prices, promotional badge banners, and feature bullets with real-time cart integration.
💳 Live Orders & Revenue Analytics
Real-time payment ledger tracking student orders, purchased packages, payment gateway IDs, student details, and revenue breakdown by class and subject.
🏫 School & Student User Directory
Manage registered institutions and students, activate/deactivate accounts, reset credentials, and review participation numbers across grades.
