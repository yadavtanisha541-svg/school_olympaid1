Frequently Asked Questions

What is OlympiadHub? ▼

OlympiadHub is a premier online examination and preparation platform hosting competitive Olympiads across Mathematics, Science, English, Cyber, and Logical Reasoning for students from Nursery to Class 10.

Who is eligible to participate? ▼

Students from any recognized school curriculum across Nursery, Kindergarten, and Classes 1 through 10 are eligible. Individual students as well as institutional school batches can participate.

How does the online proctored exam work? ▼

Exams are conducted through our secure examination engine with an interactive question palette, live countdown timer, auto-saving of answers, and security monitoring against tab switching.

How can I download my verified certificate? ▼

Immediately after exam evaluation, certificates are generated with unique Certificate IDs, score verification QR stamps, and can be downloaded or printed in PDF format from the student dashboard.
